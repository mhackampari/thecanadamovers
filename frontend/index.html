Moving Day Dos & Don'ts

✅ Do

  • Defrost and empty your fridge 24 hours before moving day
  • Label all boxes clearly with contents and destination room
  • Disassemble bed frames and flat-pack furniture ahead of time
  • Keep valuables, medications, and important documents with you personally
  • Be present and reachable on moving day
  • Confirm your elevator booking with building management
  • Prepare a tip or review if you're happy with the service

❌ Don't

  • Don't pack flammables, chemicals, or hazardous materials
  • Don't overload boxes — keep each under 50 lbs
  • Don't leave dresser drawers full for transport
  • Don't forget to update your address with Canada Post, CRA, and your bank
  • Don't book through uninsured movers to save a few dollars
  • Don't leave plants or perishables for the movers to handle last-minute
  • Don't assume parking — confirm truck access at both locations
